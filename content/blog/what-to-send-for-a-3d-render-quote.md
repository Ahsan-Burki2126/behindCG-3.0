CAD files, dielines, photos and references: what helps us quote fast and what we can work around.

## The short version

A fast, accurate quote needs three things: the product, the shots you want, and where they will be used.

## Product files

- CAD files (STEP, OBJ or FBX) are best
- Packaging dielines for labels and boxes
- Clear photos from several angles if there is no CAD
- A physical sample, if photos are not enough

## The shots

- How many images or seconds of film
- Packshot, lifestyle, detail or exploded views
- Colour and size variants
- References for the look you want

## Where it will run

Marketplaces, your store, paid social and print each have different crops and sizes. Telling us up front avoids re-renders later.

## Dates

Your launch date or deadline. If it is tight, say so: we will tell you what is realistic.
