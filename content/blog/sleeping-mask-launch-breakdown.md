How a packshot and lifestyle set came together, with the clay previews the client approved.

## The project

A skincare brand needed launch images before production samples existed. This breakdown follows the job from dieline to final render. [Owner to review.]

## From dieline to model

Dielines give exact dimensions for labels and boxes. We modelled the jar to the supplier drawing and wrapped the label artwork at its printed size.

## Clay previews

Every angle went to the client in clay first. It is the cheapest point in the project to change a composition.

## Look development

We developed the frosted glass and lid materials on one hero shot, then carried them across the set.

## Delivery

Final images were cropped for the store, marketplaces and social, from one master render each.
