## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js on Vercel (owner's choice, 2026-11-06). Commerce, course delivery and membership tooling are not chosen yet.

## Users

Two primary audiences with **equal weight**:

- **Brands and e-commerce owners** (founders, e-commerce and marketing leads) who need product visuals for stores, marketplaces and ads. They hire the studio.
- **Blender and 3D artists** who want faster workflows and better product renders. They buy addons and scene files, take courses, and join the Basic or Pro membership.

When the two pull in different directions, neither leads by default: each gets its own clear path on key pages.

## Product Purpose

Behind CG (tagline: "Visualize your products") is a 3D product visualization studio. The website exists to win client inquiries, sell Blender addons and courses, and grow a Basic/Pro membership. Success means quote requests, addon and course sales, and membership sign-ups.

## Positioning

Undecided. The owner had no preference when asked (2026-11-06). Do not state a differentiator as fact until it is confirmed. Candidates discussed: artist-built tools used on client work; e-commerce-ready visual sets; visuals plus posting automation.

## Operating Context

- Work is made in Blender, Substance Painter, Photoshop and After Effects; automations are built in n8n.
- The brand publishes on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook and Patreon.
- The studio works remotely. Its city or location must not appear on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Offerings:
1. 3D product visualization (stills, ads, e-commerce sets)
2. 3D product animation
3. Online courses on product visualization
4. Blender addons and digital products (scene files)
5. AI and social media automations

Named products and content (status to confirm before publishing claims): Batch Render Pro (published on Superhive), Clay Render Pro, Smart Texture Loader, and the free 3D Product Visualization Series on YouTube (4 episodes).

Undecided: all prices; Basic and Pro membership perks and prices, and how they relate to the existing Patreon tiers; the paid course catalogue; commerce and checkout platform.

Design-tool constraint: the Figma team is on the Starter plan (one variable mode per collection; limited MCP tool calls per month).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Behind CG. Tagline: "Visualize your products".
- Colours come from the logo (Deep Teal, Bright Teal, Orange) and the owner's contrast rules are binding; visual detail lives in DESIGN.md.
- No studio location on the site.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed yet: no publishable renders or reels supplied to the design files, no approved testimonials, no client logos with permission, no final prices.

The owner asked (2026-11-06) for easy-to-replace placeholder content in the designs for now. Every placeholder must be obviously swappable in the design files, and all of it must be replaced with real, approved material before launch. Invented testimonials, client names, logos, ratings or figures are never shipped as real.

## Product Principles

1. **Two doors, equal weight.** Every key page gives brands and artists a clear next step.
2. **Show the work.** Real renders and films are the main proof; copy supports them.
3. **Honest proof at launch.** Placeholders are for design only and get replaced before going live.
4. **Plain, specific language.** Say what is offered and who it is for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text on Orange and Bright Teal is Ink 900; on dark surfaces, teal text is Bright Teal. Touch targets at least 44px, a visible keyboard focus ring, and reduced-motion preferences respected.
